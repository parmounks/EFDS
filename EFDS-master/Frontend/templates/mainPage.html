Frequently Asked Questions

How often are the images updated? +
Our system get updated sattelite images approximately every 3 hours in Canada.
How can I receive fire alerts? +
You can subscribe to our alerts by clicking the "Subscribe" button on our homepage. Alerts will be delivered via email, and your email address will be used solely for receiving fire alerts—no promotional or sales notifications will be sent.
Can I receive an alert without having a subscription? +
No. Due to the limited number of servers available for this project, you will not receive any notification about fire if your email is not in our database.
What regions do you cover? +
We currently monitor three Canadian provinces—Alberta, British Columbia, and Ontario—due to their high fire risk, particularly during the spring and summer seasons.
How accurate are the alerts? +
Our AI system has a 98% accuracy rate verified by satellite data and ground truth reporting.
